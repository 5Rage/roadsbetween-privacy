Privacy Policy - The Roads Between

Effective date: 5 November 2026

This policy explains what information the mobile game The Roads Between ("the game") handles. The game is published by BlairsLab ("we", "us"). If you have a question, contact us at privacy@blairslab.co.nz.

Summary

  • The game collects no personal information. It has no accounts, no chat, no ads, no analytics and no purchases.
  • Your progress is saved only on your device. The game does not send it anywhere, and we run no servers that receive it.
  • We never ask for your name, email, location, contacts, photos, camera or microphone.
  • We do not sell or share personal information.

1. Information stored on your device

So you can continue your journey, the game saves on your device: your story progress, party, items, settings and which tips you have seen. This information stays in the game's private storage on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in Android settings, deletes it, and we cannot recover it.

2. Information collected by Google Play

The game is distributed through Google Play. Google may provide us with anonymous install, crash and performance reports (for example through Android vitals), depending on your device settings. These do not identify you. Google's own handling of your information is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

3. Children

The game is intended for players aged 13 and older and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone.

4. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal information about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. If you believe otherwise, or have any question, contact us at privacy@blairslab.co.nz.

5. Changes to this policy

If we change how the game handles information (for example, by adding analytics, ads or purchases), we will update this page and its date before the change takes effect.
